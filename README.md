BLOOM — Floral Studio

A refined digital storefront dedicated to flowers, floral arrangements, decorative vases, artificial botanicals, and creative floral accessories.

BLOOM is designed as a modern floral studio experience: elegant enough for home décor inspiration, practical enough for discovering tools and supplies, and organized for a smooth product-browsing experience.

🌿 Design Principles

BLOOM follows several principles throughout the storefront:

Elegant but approachable
The design should feel premium without becoming difficult to navigate.

Botanical atmosphere
Floral imagery, organic shapes, greenery, and warm neutrals establish the visual language.

Product clarity
Products should remain clearly visible and properly framed.

Editorial presentation
Floral products are presented as part of a lifestyle rather than as isolated catalog entries.

Responsive experience
The layout adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

Local-first assets
Store images and branding assets are kept locally whenever possible to reduce broken-image problems on static hosting.
